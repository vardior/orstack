# Visual checks for finalize Step 1

For visible changes, walk every acceptance criterion of the ticket or design one by one in a real browser, and record each as pass or fail with its screenshot in the scenario table; a working API, clean console and correct navigation do not show that the page looks right. Drive hover, drag and resize with real pointer input, never DOM-dispatched events. Render on data shaped like the target environment: populated charts, several badges, long names, and the empty state. Open every screenshot and check it for duplicated elements, empty or broken charts, illegible axes, overflow and misalignment before calling it a pass; synthetic-fixture screenshots alone do not count. Capture screenshots of the changed surface during live verification. When a still image cannot demonstrate an interaction claim, also capture a short recording of that interaction. Keep media in the evidence bundle and refresh affected captures after fixes. If capture is blocked, state the blocker and the unverified claim in the evidence and handoff; do not present generated HTML or logs as visual proof.
